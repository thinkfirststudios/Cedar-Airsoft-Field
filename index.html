We review the field rules before games begin. These are the ones that decide whether you play.

  1. Age

    Age 13+ recommended.

    Under 18? A guardian signs your waiver.

  2. Waiver first

    Sign before you arrive and skip the paperwork at the booth. Adults (18+) sign their own. Minors (under 18) need a guardian to sign — the guardian isn’t required to stay. Once per calendar year. All sections completed.

    Get the waiver →

  3. Eye protection

    Full seal glasses/goggles/mask (ANSI 87+) are required on the field at all times.

    No mask? Mask-only rental is $5.

    Required in staging — no exceptions.

    Cedar Airsoft barrel bags are $5 at the field.

  5. Bio BBs

    Biodegradable (bio) BBs required.

    All weights sold in person at the field store.

Chrono & engagement

Know your tag before you step out

All airsoft guns are chronoed with 0.20g (6mm) BBs and tagged with coloured zip ties before games start.

Chrono and engagement tiers. All guns chrono'd with 0.20g 6mm BBs and tagged with coloured zip ties.
TagVelocityJoulesEngagement
GreenUnder 350 FPSunder 1.1 JAny distance / 0 ft
Yellow350–400 FPS1.1–1.49 J20 ft
Red400–450 FPS1.5–1.8 J50 ftDMR / semi-locked
Clear450–520 FPS1.8–2.8 J100 ftBolt action snipers only.Not allowed at night games

Abuse of the system

We will not tolerate the abuse of the engagement system. If you are caught shooting players too close to your tag, you will receive a warning. After the first warning, players may be asked to leave the field and no refund will be issued.

Re-chrono

If you would like to change the spring or your gun, or the pressure of your HPA tank, let us know and we will gladly re-chrono you.

Field rules

The basics

  • No shooting in staging! Guns on safe, mag out, barrel bags on. HPA lines disconnected, pistols holstered.
  • Full seal glasses/goggles/mask (ANSI 87+) are required on the field at all times.
  • No blind fire.
  • Call your hits. Don’t overshoot.
  • We have no “bang” rule! An airsoft BB must make contact with the target to count as a hit.
  • Grenades have a 15 ft kill radius. Check with a ref before using grenades or pyro.

Waiver

Sign before you arrive

Waivers can be filled out at the field, or printed and filled out at home and brought with you. Doing it at home gets you through admissions faster.

  • Adults (18+) can fill out the waiver on their own.
  • Minors (under 18) must have a guardian fill out the waiver. The guardian is not required to stay for the duration of the event.
  • Waivers only need to be filled out once per calendar year.
  • All sections of the waiver must be filled out.
