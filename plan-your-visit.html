Pack list

What to bring & what’s provided

Bring

  • A signed waiver — or fill one out at the admissions booth
  • Full seal glasses/goggles/mask (ANSI 87+)
  • A barrel bag — required in staging
  • Bio BBs only
  • Your own water bottle
  • Military ID, for the $15 rate
  • A driver’s license or car keys as collateral, if renting

Provided at the field

  • Free water
  • Rental packages — $20
  • Mask-only rental — $5
  • Field store — bio BBs, gas, eye pro, merch
  • HPA fills — $5 all day (game days, or call ahead)
  • Pizza — 2 slices + a sports drink, $5
  • Chrono and coloured zip-tie tags before games start
  • A rules review before games begin

First time?

Your first game, step by step

  1. Pick a date

    Check Events for the next rec day or night game.

  2. Sign the waiver

    At home or at the booth. Adults (18+) sign their own; minors (under 18) need a guardian to sign — the guardian isn’t required to stay. Once per calendar year; all sections completed.

  3. Gear up

    Full seal glasses/goggles/mask (ANSI 87+) are required on the field at all times.

    No gear? Rent a kit for $20.

  4. Chrono

    Every gun is chronoed with 0.20g BBs and tagged before games start. See the tiers.

  5. Play

    We review the field rules, then games begin. Call your hits.

Age 13+ recommended.
