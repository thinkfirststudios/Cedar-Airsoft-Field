What to expect

  • A waiver must be completed and signed before playing — at the admissions booth on the day, or printed ahead of time. One waiver is good for the entire calendar year.
  • Admission is $20. Water is free (bring your own bottle). $5 gets you 2 slices of pizza and a sports drink.
  • Rental packages are available for those without their own gear.
  • We chrono guns with coloured zip ties before games start, with 0.20g BBs.
  • We review the field rules before games begin.
  • Please clean up after yourself and your area in staging before leaving the event.
