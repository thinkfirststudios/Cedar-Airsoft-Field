Eye protection#

Full seal glasses/goggles/mask (ANSI 87+) are required on the field at all times.Field rule

No mask? A mask-only rental is $5, and full seal goggles ($28) and high performance goggles ($35) are sold at the field store.

Staging#

No shooting in staging!Field rule
  • Guns must be on safe with the mag out and barrel bags on while in staging.
  • HPA lines disconnected.
  • Pistols holstered.
  • Barrel bags are required in staging — no exceptions. Cedar Airsoft barrel bags are available for $5.

Chrono & engagement#

All airsoft guns are chronoed with 0.20g (6mm) BBs and tagged with coloured zip ties before games start.

Chrono and engagement tiers. All guns chrono'd with 0.20g 6mm BBs and tagged with coloured zip ties.

We will not tolerate the abuse of the engagement system. If you are caught shooting players too close to your tag, you will receive a warning. After the first warning, players may be asked to leave the field and no refund will be issued.

If you would like to change the spring or your gun, or the pressure of your HPA tank, let us know and we will gladly re-chrono you.

Gameplay#

  • No blind fire.
  • Don’t overshoot. Listen to other players to call out “hit”, have their hand up, or use a dead rag.
  • Call your hits. We understand some hits may not be felt due to a weaker gun, a far shot, or many layers (especially during winter). Intentional ignoring of hits is not tolerated.
We have no “bang” rule! An airsoft BB must make contact with the target to count as a hit.Field rule

Grenades & pyro#

Grenades have a 15 ft kill radius. Check with a ref before using grenades or pyro.Field rule

Bio BBs#

Biodegradable (bio) BBs required.Field rule

Bio BBs of all weights are available in person at our field store.

The property#

  • Clean up after yourself.
  • Do not destroy, move, or vandalize the property.

Age & waiver#

Age 13+ recommended.Field rule
  • Adults (18+) can fill out the waiver on their own.
  • Minors (under 18) must have a guardian fill out the waiver. The guardian is not required to stay for the duration of the event.
  • Waivers only need to be filled out once per calendar year.
  • All sections of the waiver must be filled out.

Rentals#

  • Rental rifles are semi-automatic M4s at 300–350 FPS (0 ft engagement / green tag).
  • Please only use our BBs with our rentals.
  • We collect collateral — something we can hold onto, such as a driver’s license or car keys — for each kit rented out. No exceptions.
